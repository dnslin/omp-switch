- Pixel inspection and the runtime normalization guard found every RGBA pixel in the extra band equal to `[248,249,252,255]`, the page background; no content crosses the boundary. Normalization therefore excludes only this proven, uniform bottom backing-surface band. A non-uniform extra band fails the test rather than being cropped.
